COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Lex Covenant and legal resources.

What is Lex Covenant?

Lex Covenant is an independent legal information and knowledge platform.

Is Lex Covenant affiliated with the Kerala High Court?

No. Lex Covenant is an independent platform and is not the official website of the Kerala High Court.

Can I check my Kerala High Court case here?

Lex Covenant can guide you to relevant official resources. Always use the official court systems for authoritative case information.

Can Lex Covenant provide legal advice?

The platform is designed primarily for legal information and education. Specific legal advice should be obtained from a qualified legal professional.

Where can I find legal aid in Kerala?

Lex Covenant can help users locate relevant legal-aid resources and authorities.

Can Lex Covenant explain a court notice?

General information can help explain terminology and processes, but a qualified legal professional should review documents when specific legal advice is required.
