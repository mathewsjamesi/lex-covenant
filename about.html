ABOUT

Legal knowledge, organized for practical use.

Lex Covenant is an independent legal information platform designed to make legal concepts, procedures, resources and public information easier to understand.

What Lex Covenant does

We organize legal information into practice areas, resources, articles, legal terms and practical guides so users can understand the basic structure of a legal issue before deciding what to do next.

Kerala focus

The platform is designed with a Kerala-oriented public information layer, including links and information pathways relating to the Kerala High Court, Kerala Judiciary, government resources and legal aid.

What it is not

Lex Covenant is not the official website of the Kerala High Court, a government department, or a substitute for advice from a qualified advocate. Information on this website should be independently verified before being relied upon.
