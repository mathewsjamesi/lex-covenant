FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Basic questions about Lex Covenant and the information available on the platform.

Is Lex Covenant the official Kerala High Court website?

No. Lex Covenant is an independent legal information platform. Official court information should be obtained from the relevant official court source.

Does Lex Covenant provide legal advice?

The platform provides general legal information and educational material. It does not replace advice from a qualified advocate who can consider the facts of a specific matter.

Can I use the information to handle my case myself?

General information can help you understand a process, but legal procedures and deadlines can be fact-specific. Verify important information and seek professional advice where appropriate.

Does Lex Covenant cover only Kerala?

The initial focus is Kerala-oriented legal information and public resources, while the platform can expand to broader Indian legal topics.

Can AI answer my legal questions?

AI can help explain legal concepts and organize information, but AI-generated information should be verified against authoritative legal sources and should not be treated as a substitute for professional legal advice.
